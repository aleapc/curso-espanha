Quiz — Money manners and the con

Las Ramblas, a sprig of rosemary, two beers and a charge that was never on the menu

🎬 Barcelona, late afternoon. A woman offers a 'gift' on Las Ramblas; then a terrace, two cañas, and a bill with a cover charge nobody mentioned.

1. A woman steps in front of him on Las Ramblas and presses a sprig of rosemary into his hand, calling it a gift. What does he say — and what do his feet do?

2. The waitress says 'Son dieciséis con cincuenta.' How much is the bill?

3. A number on the bill looks wrong. What does he ask for FIRST?

4. What is 'el cubierto' — and why does he object to it?

5. 'La hoja de reclamaciones, por favor.' Why does that sentence end the argument?

6. According to UK Foreign Office advice, a genuine Spanish police officer will ask to see…

7. The bill lands at fourteen euros, he hands over fifteen and says 'Está bien así.' Is that the right amount to leave?
