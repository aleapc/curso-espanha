Quiz — Getting around

Airport to hotel to the fast train, without being touted, overcharged or left behind

🎬 Arrivals hall at Madrid Barajas: a tout, then the taxi rank, the fixed fare and the receipt. The next morning: the airport supplement at the ticket window, the access gate at Atocha, the track number and the luggage frame.

1. A man in the arrivals hall offers her a cheap taxi and she walks straight past him. Why?

2. What does she settle BEFORE the car moves?

3. She asks '¿Este billete vale para el aeropuerto?'. What does the clerk tell her?

4. At Atocha she opens with 'Ya tengo el billete'. What is that sentence buying her?

5. 'Sale a las once, vía doce.' When does the train go, and from where?

6. Why does the guard tell her to go through 'ya' — right now — when the train isn't for a while?

7. 'Si pasa por el arco, sí.' What decides whether her suitcase can come along?
