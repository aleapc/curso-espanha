Quiz — The Spanish clock and the bar dossier

Twenty past seven, a neighbourhood bar, and everything from episode one arriving at once

🎬 A traveller walks into a neighbourhood bar at 19:20, finds the kitchen closed, orders a beer at the bar instead, checks the prices and the tapa, books a table for half nine and pays.

1. It's twenty past seven. What does she tell him about food?

2. The barwoman's first word to him is 'Dime'. What is she doing?

3. He asks '¿Es el mismo precio en la barra?'. What does she tell him?

4. Does the tapa come free with the beer in this bar?

5. What time does he book the table for?

6. She asks '¿Cuántos sois?'. What does she want to know?

7. 'Son tres euros con cincuenta.' How much is the beer?
