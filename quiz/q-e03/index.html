Quiz — Bar, table and the bill

One evening in a Madrid bar, from the door to the last euro

🎬 A couple walks into a Spanish bar in the evening: they greet, check whether the terrace costs more, order drinks, ask for the menu, sort out portion sizes, get tap water, decline the bread, ask for the bill and query a charge.

1. The barman says «Dime». What is he doing?

2. Why does she ask «¿Es el mismo precio en la barra?» before sitting down?

3. She asks for «la carta». What would she have got if she'd asked for «el menú»?

4. The barman says the octopus is a «ración». Why does she switch to «media ración»?

5. She orders «una jarra de agua del grifo». What's true about that in Spain?

6. The total is «veinticinco con cincuenta». How much is that?

7. «Perdone, creo que hay un error… esto no lo hemos pedido.» If the waiter still refuses, what's the next step?
